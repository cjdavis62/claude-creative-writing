# Decisions

Append-only log of canon and process decisions, with the reasoning behind each. When a decision is revised, leave the old entry in place and add a new one referencing it. Entries are dated.

---

## 2026-05-01 — Working title: *The Doors of Gods*
**Decision:** *The Doors of Gods*.
**Reasoning:** Resonates with the gateway mechanic. "Doors" reads slightly more grammatically and tonally interesting than "Gates"; the plural "Gods" foregrounds plurality (council societies, the variety of divine presences). Held provisionally per `PROJECT_PLAN.md` §7; revisit after Phase 2.

## 2026-05-01 — Author byline: C. Davis and A. Claude
**Decision:** **C. Davis and A. Claude.**
**Reasoning:** Dual byline that is clear about the human/AI collaboration without belaboring it. Reads alphabetically, which gives the byline a natural rhythm.

## 2026-05-01 — First deliverable: fragment dossier
**Decision:** Four to six short fragments (500–1500 words each) set in this world but adjacent to the main plot, paired with a `QUESTIONS.md` log of questions arising during drafting.
**Reasoning:** Defers commitment to a longer form; exercises the agent pipeline on real prose; populates the wiki with concrete texture; surfaces voice questions in low-stakes contexts. See `PROJECT_PLAN.md` §3.4.

## 2026-05-01 — First fragment: an emergence witnessed by a child
**Decision:** Author's pick from the dossier candidates.
**Reasoning:** Engages the brainstorm's "founding moment before interpretation" theme (`brainstorm/god_society_dynamics_v3_1.md` §I); gives a specific concrete lens on the emergence mechanic; tight focal POV for testing voice in a low-overhead form.

## 2026-05-01 — Wiki structure
**Decision:** Top-level folders `wiki/characters/`, `wiki/locations/`, `wiki/concepts/`, `wiki/societies/`, `wiki/timeline/`. New folders added as the wiki demands them; the first creation of any new folder is announced rather than silent.
**Reasoning:** Per `PROJECT_PLAN.md` §7. Flat per-entity markdown is sufficient at this scale; the lore agents can grep across files without need for a vector store.

## 2026-05-01 — Pen name vs. real name
**Decision:** Pen name (C. Davis and A. Claude) rather than real name.
**Reasoning:** Author's preference per `PROJECT_PLAN.md` §7. Acknowledges the collaboration honestly without making it the entire identity of the work.
