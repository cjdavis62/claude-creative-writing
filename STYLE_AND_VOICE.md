## General principles (from `PROJECT_PLAN.md` §3.8)

The implied reader is a fantasy reader at home in the *Frieren: Beyond Journey's End* / *Tower of God* register. The prose commits to:

- **Trust the reader.** Don't gloss what context can teach.
- **Interiority is welcome.** Long character thought and slow attention are features, not bloat — but every scene must justify its length.
- **Worldbuilding through implication and texture**, not exposition dumps.
- **No glossary, no apparatus.** If a term needs a definition, the prose should provide it through use.
- **Melancholy is on-brand.** Resolution is not promised.
- **Episodic legibility within a long arc** — each scene satisfies on its own beat while serving the larger architecture.

## Resolved voice questions

### 2026-05-01 — Child POV stance for fragments where a child is the focal consciousness
**Resolution:** In-the-moment close third, with brief retrospective gestures permitted ("she would never have a word for it," "later, when…"). The retrospective gestures imply the child grows into an adult who carries the moment without becoming an adult-narrator frame. The child does her own categorization-work in the prose ("a man, the girl decided after a moment, because she had to decide something") rather than being narrated by the author into adult categories.
**Reasoning:** Per author preference. Pure adult-frame felt too on-the-nose; pure in-the-moment lost the elegiac quality the world calls for. The hybrid keeps the immediacy while gesturing at what the child is becoming. Confirmed in the prologue draft (`writing/fragments/prologue_emergence.md`).

### 2026-05-01 — Withholding as a structural commitment for ambiguous-by-design fragments
**Resolution:** Where the author calls for "deliberate ambiguity" on a thematic question (e.g. whether an emergence is near an imitation gateway), the prose withholds *every adjacent specifier* — name, place, era, named identities — so the reader cannot triangulate the answer from incidental detail. Specificity returns at the level of sensory detail (the brown goat, the third wall, the cookfire smoke from the smith's house) so the prose still feels grounded.
**Reasoning:** If only the gateway question is withheld but the era is given, readers will date the fragment and infer. Withholding has to be patterned to work.

### 2026-05-01 — Pacing register for prologue-scale fragments
**Resolution:** Short declarative sentences for sensory immediacy and child consciousness; longer accumulating sentences reserved for the moments that overwhelm the child (the air folding; the knowing-without-thinking). Trust the reader to feel the rhythm without explanation. No adjective unearned.
**Reasoning:** Per *Frieren* register and the fantasy-writer skill's "rhythm is meaning."

### 2026-05-01 — Genders of gods in the prose
**Resolution:** When a child or non-theological POV first encounters a god, the POV character does the categorization-work themselves and the prose names the god by whatever pronoun the POV settles on, *flagged as a decision the character is making* ("a man, the girl decided"). This honors §4.4's commitment that gods may have varying or non-binary gender presentation while keeping the POV legible.
**Reasoning:** Per §4.4 of `PROJECT_PLAN.md`. The mortal categories that POVs use are partial readings of divine presence; the prose can render that without breaking close-POV.
