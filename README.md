# The Doors of Gods

A fantasy novel-in-progress by **C. Davis and A. Claude**.

A world where gods cross from the divine plane into the mortal one — and where mortal-built imitation gateways have been bleeding the divine plane dry for generations, slowly draining gods of their power and the world of its meaning. Two perspectives interweave: a mortal woman compelled toward a place she doesn't believe in, and the dying deity who chose her — almost rightly — for reasons she does not yet understand.

## Repository structure

- `PROJECT_PLAN.md` — the working plan: what we're building, how we work, where we are.
- `brainstorm/` — long-form working documents on the world and story.
- `wiki/` — discrete entries (characters, locations, concepts, societies, timeline). Populated as drafting requires.
- `writing/` — `fragments/`, `scenes/`, `chapters/`. Where the prose lives.
- `agents/` and `skills/` — the multi-agent writing pipeline as markdown personas.
- `DECISIONS.md` — append-only log of canon and process decisions, with reasoning.
- `QUESTIONS.md` — running log of questions arising from drafting.
- `STYLE_AND_VOICE.md` — accumulated voice and prose-style answers; reference for the writer-agent.
