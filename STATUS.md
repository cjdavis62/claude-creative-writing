## The world in one paragraph

Two planes, mortal and divine. Gods cross into the mortal world through visible threshold-doors; mortals, out of devotion, built imitation gateways that run the mechanism in reverse — slowly draining the divine plane toward collapse. No one understands this is happening. The protagonist is gateway-marked (drained, narrowed, sees the world stripped of idealism) and doesn't know it. The dying deity reads her mark as useful clarity and charges her with a quest she doesn't want. The last god's emergence is an expulsion, not an arrival. The catastrophe was built out of love.
