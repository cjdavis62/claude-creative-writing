# Relen

A lay trader operating out of Vanaek. Regular at the Vaelen. Has known Sorin long enough that their usual pattern is legible to her — she notices deviations without tracking them consciously.

## What is established

- Manages grain shipments (at least one ongoing, with weight problems)
- Uses the Vaelen as a working space when waiting for contacts or couriers
- Pays attention: she noticed that Sorin's robe was damp from rain, not from interior work; she noticed the prepared absence of reaction to the late bell
- Does not push when she notices things. Keeps her voice even. Goes home.

## What is withheld

- What she does with what she observes
- Her history in Vanaek and her relationship to the council structure (lay, pragmatic — but how much does she know about what's happening?)
- Whether she has connections outside Vanaek (the courier network suggests she might)

## Notes

Relen is not gateway-marked and is not the protagonist. She is an example of what careful, unillusioned attention looks like in a functioning person — someone who reads the world accurately and then continues about her business. Her voice register (economical, specific, not lyrical) is the close-third adult counterpart to the prologue's first-person child. Future fragments may or may not return to her.
