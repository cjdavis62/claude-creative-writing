# Relen

A factor operating out of Vanaek: she manages supply chains on behalf of merchants and institutions, not on her own account. Her professional value is reliability — she is the person who knows who to call, who will show up, and how to make arrangements hold. When informal coordination infrastructure breaks down, it attacks the substrate of everything she does for a living.

## What is established

- Manages grain shipments and similar logistics on behalf of clients (not her own stock)
- Uses the Vaelen as a working space when waiting for contacts, couriers, or counterparties
- Pays attention: she noticed Sorin's robe was damp from rain, not interior work; she noticed the prepared absence of reaction; she read the accord failures in the room before she had language for them
- Does not push when she notices things. Keeps her voice even. Goes home.
- Has a courier relationship with **Beket**, who has run the eastern road (Vanaek → Solaven region) for fifteen years. Relen knows Beket by reliability and track record rather than personal closeness. "She does not stop for slow roads."
- The grain handoff going sideways — Imek not present, the substitute not knowing the arrangement, three days of messages for what should have taken an afternoon — is not an ordinary mishap. It is the accord domain's failure expressing itself as professional erosion. Relen is in the path of what she's watching.

## What is withheld

- What she does with what she observes — whether she tells anyone, files it, acts on it
- Her history in Vanaek and relationship to the council structure (lay, pragmatic — but how embedded?)
- The extent to which she has begun to connect the pattern (grain handoff, Sorin's silence, the warehouse negotiation) into something she would call a conclusion
- Whether the courier network she uses is also the channel through which readers of decline travel

## Stakes

Relen's livelihood is a direct casualty of the accord domain's failure: her work depends on people being in the right place at the right time, arrangements being honored, informal coordination holding. As the accord god declines, her professional reputation erodes through failures she cannot explain or prevent. She is not yet naming this. The fragment catches her in the early stage of noticing, before she has the framework to see what she's noticing.

## The courier thread

Beket's eastern road connects Vanaek to the broader eastern network including Solaven's region. The commercial courier routes and the word-of-mouth channels that readers of decline use to identify sites worth observing run along the same physical roads. Relen and Tavoen (see [[tavoen]]) do not know each other, but they are connected through the same infrastructure — the roads that carry grain contracts also carry the signals that bring readers to their observation posts.

## Notes

Relen is not gateway-marked and is not the protagonist. She is an example of what careful, unillusioned attention looks like in a functioning person — someone who reads the world accurately and then continues about her business. Her voice register (economical, specific, not lyrical) is the close-third adult counterpart to the prologue's first-person child. The accord domain's failure is personal for her in a way the fragment doesn't yet name: she is professionally dependent on the coordination infrastructure that is quietly failing around her. Future fragments may or may not return to her.
