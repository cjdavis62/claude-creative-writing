# ComfyUI-Ready Prompts — The Doors of Gods

Style preamble used on all prompts: **`Ink and watercolor illustration.`** — prepend this to every prompt. Flux understands natural language well so these are written as sentences, not tags.

**Workflow settings** (use `doors-of-gods-flux-workflow.json`):
- Steps: 20 | CFG: 1.0 | Sampler: euler | Scheduler: simple

## Concepts & Locations

### Emergence

**The Threshold** `1216×832`
```
Ink and watercolor illustration. A field at dusk. In the center, a threshold stands in the open air — upright, door-shaped, roughly as wide as two outstretched arms and taller than a person. It is not made of anything. It is simply a thinning of the air in the shape of a door. Through it, a glimpse of somewhere else: a horizon that does not match the surrounding landscape, light that moves differently, a sky of uncertain color. At the base of the threshold, several goats stand absolutely still, facing it — not afraid, not grazing. Waiting. The surrounding field in muted watercolor, the threshold in fine ink linework against pale luminous wash. Blue-grey dusk, pale gold at the threshold, dark forms of the goats. Fantasy book illustration style.
```

**First Witness** `1216×832`
```
Ink and watercolor illustration. A child standing in a field, looking up at something. She is young — seven or eight — and her body language is very still, the way children go still when something is outside their categories. Around her feet: goat hoofprints in soft earth. Just above the horizon, the suggestion of a threshold already closing — a shimmer, a fold in the air, more felt than seen. At the very edge of the frame, faint, the suggestion of a figure who has just arrived: immaculate clothing, hands held slightly away from the body, eyes just beginning to focus. The child is the center. The threshold and arriving figure are peripheral. Loose watercolor in pale morning light, fine ink detail on the child's expression — not afraid, present. Cool grey-blue with pale gold where the threshold was. Fantasy book illustration style.
```

### Decline

**The Accord Domain Failing** `1216×832`
```
Ink and watercolor illustration. A crowded marketplace or gathering hall, rendered in a slightly dissonant way: people are present, conversations are happening, commerce is occurring — but none of it is landing. Two figures reach to shake hands at a slightly wrong moment. A third person speaks to a fourth who responds a beat too late. A deal that should have closed three days ago has not. The scene looks busy from a distance; up close, it is quietly, unnamably off. Warm amber and ochre palette — a living busy place — but the ink linework draws out the gaps: the pauses, the missed beats, the conversations that keep not finding rhythm. No supernatural effect visible. Fantasy book illustration style, visible brushwork.
```

**The Imitation Gateway** `832×1216`
```
Ink and watercolor illustration. An ancient sacred site — a stone threshold set into a hillside, carved with devotional marks, worn smooth by generations of hands. Offerings are arranged at its base. Someone kneels before it in prayer. The threshold is real, well-made, clearly the result of love and craft and faith. But from a slightly elevated perspective, the scene shows something the worshipper cannot see: the air above the threshold trembles very faintly, moving not toward the divine but away from it, a slow invisible bleed. The worshipper does not notice. Nothing looks wrong from the ground. The painting holds both truths at once: an act of sincere devotion, and the mechanism of catastrophe. Warm amber for the devotion, fine ink for the threshold's carved detail, faint almost-invisible distortion above. Fantasy book illustration style.
```

### Wild Gods

**Landscape as God** `1920×1088`
```
Ink and watercolor landscape. A vast eastern wilderness — deep forest, high ground, a valley where sound is wrong. In the far distance, something large is suggested: not a figure exactly, more a quality of the terrain — a part of the landscape that is denser, older, more present than the rest, and toward which light and sound are subtly directed. Birds in the foreground are mid-flight but angled inward. A stream moves not toward the viewer but away, into the mass of the distance. A traveler at the very edge of the frame has stopped walking, not in fear but because their spatial sense no longer works reliably. The god is the landscape. The landscape does not know the traveler is there. Deep green and grey watercolor, heavy ink linework for trees and stones, the distant center in lighter less-defined wash. Fantasy book illustration style.
```

## Usage Notes

- **Vary the seed** for different interpretations of the same prompt — Flux Dev generates meaningfully different results across seeds.
- **Flux reads long prose well** — don't shorten these prompts to tags; the narrative detail is what makes the results distinctive.
- **Save good seeds** — when a result captures the character correctly, record the seed in the character's wiki page so you can do variations from the same starting point.
- **Iterating on a result** — reduce `denoise` to 0.5–0.7 and use img2img to refine a nearly-right image without losing its composition.
