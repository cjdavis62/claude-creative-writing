# Character Concept Art Prompts

Prompts for DALL-E / ChatGPT. Style target: **ink and watercolor illustration** — loose, hand-drawn, evocative rather than photorealistic. Think fantasy book illustration with visible brushwork and ink linework.

Each prompt includes the image text, a note on what the prompt is trying to capture, and a suggested aspect ratio.

---

## The Protagonist (unnamed)

### Portrait — The Watcher

**Prompt:**
> An ink and watercolor illustration of a young woman standing at the edge of a dusty road, looking at something just out of frame with an expression that is neither afraid nor hopeful — simply attentive. She is lean, plainly dressed for travel, worn in a practical way. Her face is striking not for its beauty but for its quality of attention: she sees things, and does not soften what she sees. Behind her, in loose watercolor wash, a village recedes — low walls, a pale sky. The illustration uses muted earth tones with occasional sharp ink detail in her eyes and hands. The mood is gritty and clear. No idealism. No ornamentation.

**What this captures:** The protagonist's core register — body-first attention, unillusioned perception. The gateway-mark as stripped clarity rather than visible damage.

**Aspect ratio:** Portrait (2:3)

### Portrait — The Gateway Mark

**Prompt:**
> An ink and watercolor illustration of a young woman's face and upper body, rendered in close detail. She appears to be in the presence of something significant — a faint luminescence at the edge of the frame that she does not look toward. Her expression is closed, inward, slightly narrowed, as if the light costs her something. Around her, suggestion of a ruined stone threshold, half-buried in grass. The watercolor palette is deliberately desaturated — grays and dusty ochres — except at the very edges of the frame, where pale gold bleeds in. Fine ink linework traces the tension in her jaw and the set of her shoulders.

**What this captures:** The protagonist's relationship to the divine plane — she carries the evidence of the gateway drain in her body, without knowing it. The image should feel like something is being taken from her at the edges.

**Aspect ratio:** Portrait (2:3)

## The Dying Deity

### At the Tree — The Commission

**Prompt:**
> An ink and watercolor illustration of a figure leaning against the trunk of an enormous, ancient tree. He appears to be a man in late middle age but feels older in a way that is hard to place — something in the quality of the light around him is wrong, too bright without a visible source. His clothing is fine but slightly displaced, as if he arrived without quite expecting to. His hands rest open at his sides. He is looking at someone just outside the frame with an expression of deep earnestness — the look of someone who has thought very carefully and still cannot quite explain what he knows. The tree's roots sprawl around him; loose watercolor in pale gold and grey-blue renders both his form and the tree with a luminous softness. Ink linework is minimal — used only for the most specific details: the lines of his hands, the bark's texture, the quality of his gaze.

**What this captures:** The dying deity at the moment of the novel's opening — earnest, declining, luminous with a quality that is also a kind of losing. The tree as the place where the quest begins.

**Aspect ratio:** Landscape (3:2)

### Study — The Luminous Wound

**Prompt:**
> An ink and watercolor portrait of an aging man whose perception of the world is too beautiful. He stands near a window and looks out at something — a street, a courtyard, an ordinary view — with an expression of wondering attention, as if what he sees is unbearably vivid. The world outside the window is rendered in rich, almost oversaturated watercolor, slightly blurred, like a painting of a painting. His own form is crisp and grounded by ink. He does not look sad. He looks the way someone looks when they are seeing something that will, in moments, be gone. The palette is warm gold and cool blue-grey, the ink work delicate.

**What this captures:** The dying deity's perceptual wound — he sees the world as pure ideal, beautiful and blind at once. The luminosity that increases as he declines.

**Aspect ratio:** Portrait (2:3)

## Relen

### At the Vaelen — The Factor at Work

**Prompt:**
> An ink and watercolor illustration of a woman in her thirties or forties, sitting alone at a table in a busy inn or tavern. She is not eating or drinking — she has a ledger open, a small writing kit beside it, and she is writing with quiet precision. Around her, the room hums with muted activity — other tables, low candlelight, figures in conversation rendered in loose watercolor wash. She is in focus; everyone else is slightly not. Her face is observant, composed, neither warm nor cold — the face of someone who is always paying attention and has decided, for now, not to act on what she notices. Ink linework is strongest around her pen, her hands, her steady expression. The palette is warm amber and deep brown, with the room around her in softer, greyer tones.

**What this captures:** Relen's professional composure and careful attention. She is the person who reads the room before anyone else and then returns to her ledger.

**Aspect ratio:** Landscape (3:2)

## Tavoen

### At the Ritual — The Watcher

**Prompt:**
> An ink and watercolor illustration of a figure standing at the back of a candlelit hall, slightly apart from the crowd of worshippers. They are dressed to blend in — plain, layered clothing, no distinguishing marks — but their body language is subtly different: they are not a participant. They are watching. In one hand, concealed against their hip, a small merchant's ledger. Their gaze is directed at the front of the hall, where a ritual is in progress — figures around an altar, soft light, the suggestion of ceremony. The crowd is rendered in loose, undifferentiated watercolor. Tavoen is rendered in careful ink detail: the angle of their jaw, the stillness of their posture, the quality of absolute attention. The palette is deep blue-green candlelight, shadow.

**What this captures:** Tavoen's fanatical methodology — present in the hall, invisible in the crowd, counting.

**Aspect ratio:** Landscape (3:2)

### Study — The Count

**Prompt:**
> An ink and watercolor close study of two hands holding a small ledger open to a page of entries — numbers, tallies, a shorthand that looks like merchant notation. The handwriting is precise and small. Around the edges of the page, faint shadows suggest a candlelit room. The ledger is worn, the pages slightly wavy from use. No face is visible — only the hands and the page. Ink linework is strongest at the written entries; the hands and background are loose watercolor. The palette is warm amber-brown and deep shadow.

**What this captures:** The method of the reader of decline — the count as vocation, the evidence gathered by obsessive attention.

**Aspect ratio:** Square (1:1) or Portrait (2:3)

## Sorin

### At the Vaelen — The Still Point

**Prompt:**
> An ink and watercolor illustration of a figure sitting alone at a corner table in a busy tavern-like gathering space. They are in clergy-adjacent attire — not elaborate, but clearly of a specific role. A leather satchel leans against their chair. They are conspicuously still: while the room around them moves and murmurs, they sit with hands folded, pausing before each small action — picking up a cup, turning their head — as if checking each movement against an internal calculation. The room around them is rendered in warm, busy watercolor, its rhythms slightly off: a couple whose conversation isn't landing, a deal that won't close. Sorin is the still center of a room that has lost its beat. Fine ink detail on their composed expression; softer watercolor for their form. Palette: warm amber, grey-green, shadow.

**What this captures:** Sorin holding their own coordination manually while the accord god's domain fails around them — present, deliberate, the attendant of a declining god.

**Aspect ratio:** Landscape (3:2)
